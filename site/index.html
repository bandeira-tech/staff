§ 01

Three principles, no negotiation.

The frame we build STAFF inside of. The bias on every call.

  1. 01

    Meet them where they are.

    The strongest process is an improvement on a builder’s own organic flow. We don’t enforce your starting points. We ensure you capture value and evolve with it.

    You move. We organize.

  2. 02

    Compound real success.

    Progress is small, confident steps. Value built over time at a pace we can consume and prove — not by one-shotting a mess that burns context and trust.

    Small steps. Real ground.

  3. 03

    Your data is yours.

    Information you generate should be useful and reusable wherever else you need it. Not locked in. Not hard to access. Plain files you can read, edit, replicate.

    The user is free.

§ 02

Atomic primitives.
Not monoliths.

Subagents and skills are big, opinionated, and brittle. STAFF breaks them apart. Smaller blast radius. Gradual adoption. Cheap to modify and test. Easy to capture as you build.

SHIPPING

Traits

Portable building blocks. Focused information and guidance over a limited, specific context. Compose them into a tighter profile when a moment demands it.

/traits/newbie/MAIN.md trait

doesn’t know enough about the universal data availability layer

believes data should be sent to REST APIs and databases

Traits keep you out of monolithic agents that are hard to update and eat valuable context. Improve the model, keep the work.

SHIPPING

Plays

Reusable workflows. Agents cooperate over a shared objective with expected phases, gates and outputs. Compose with any traits.

  1. 01crazy8s→ sketches
  2. 02public prototype→ feedback
  3. 03retrospective→ report

Plays are how agents agree on shape before they argue about content. Phases. Gates. Outputs. The same play, different traits, new work.

SOON

Positions

Named seats in your operation. Traits attach to positions. Agents fill them. The role outlives any single agent.

SOON

Teams

Positions grouped under shared plays and shared context. The unit you task with an objective and watch unfold.

§ 03

Your data, on your disk.

STAFF doesn’t hoard. It mounts wherever you point it and writes plain markdown — navigable, editable, and easy to replicate to any target storage you trust.

What this gives you

  • i. Readable on its own. Markdown. cd in, open, read. No client required.
  • ii. Mountable anywhere. Your dotfiles, a vault, a project folder, a synced drive.
  • iii. Replicable. An HTTP server, S3, or another rig — point and copy.
  • iv. Reusable by other agents. Other tools can read these primitives directly.

STAFF is a b3nd application, sibling to b3nd-cc-chat. The product is the framework. The data is yours.

§ 05

STAFF vs. Agents & Skills.

Why atomic beats monolithic when the model under you keeps changing.

The old way

Agents & Skills

  • Large, opinionated, hard to modify.
  • Big context cost per call.
  • Lock-in to a single model snapshot.
  • High blast radius on small edits.
The STAFF way

Traits & Plays

  • Small, portable, composable.
  • Cheap to test, cheap to replace.
  • Move with model improvements, not against them.
  • Gradual adoption, evolution, capture.
